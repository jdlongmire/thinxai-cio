Straight answers

The questions a careful buyer should ask.

How are you different from my MSP's vCIO?

An MSP earns margin selling and operating products. When the same firm claims to be your independent strategic adviser, the incentive is embedded. This practice takes no commissions and no referral fees, discloses any prior vendor relationships in writing, and gets competing bids on material purchases. The advice and the product sale are structurally separated.

Does AI make the decisions?

No. AI accelerates research, monitoring, drafting, and reporting — the work that makes continuous governance affordable for a mid-market firm. Every recommendation is made and owned by a human executive. Nothing material is decided autonomously.

Who owns our data and the work product?

You do. Roadmaps, decision records, evidence indexes, and documentation are yours, in exportable form, from day one. There is no hostage documentation.

What does "evidence-producing" actually mean?

Intended action, authority, execution, result, evaluation — recorded as work happens, not reconstructed at quarter-end. Your leadership, your insurer, your customers, and your auditors can inspect the same record.

We're smaller than 20 people / larger than 150. Still a fit?

Possibly. The assessment offers are the right starting point — if the diagnostic doesn't justify a retainer, you'll still have the facts and the plan. The honest answer comes from the diagnostic, not a sales call.
